## Notes

- Pay is low. Averages of £100-300 per job mean the value is in reusing one
  pipeline across many similar jobs, not in any single gig.
- Many listings are for India, US, Ireland or Australia tax work. I skipped those.
- Filing, payroll and tax-return jobs need a qualified person to sign off.
- One listing asked for fabricated bank statements. That is fraud, so avoid it.
- Upwork pages returned 403 to me, and PeoplePerHour pages loaded empty, so I
  couldn't check live postings there. Search "invoice", "PDF to Excel" and
  "reconciliation", sorted by newest.
- A PeoplePerHour data-entry job I found (800 transactions for £80) was already awarded.
